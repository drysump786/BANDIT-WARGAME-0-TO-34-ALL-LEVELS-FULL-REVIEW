A program is running automatically at regular intervals from **cron**, the time-based job scheduler. Look in **/etc/cron.d/** for the configuration and see what command is being executed.

## Commands you may need to solve this level

cron, crontab, crontab(5) (use “man 5 crontab” to access this)

```
bandit21@bandit:~$ ls -a
.  ..  .bash_logout  .bashrc  .prevpass  .profile

bandit21@bandit:~$ cd /etc/cron.d

bandit21@bandit:/etc/cron.d$ ls -a
.   .placeholder       clean_tmp         cronjob_bandit23  e2scrub_all         manpage3_resetpw_job
..  behemoth4_cleanup  cronjob_bandit22  cronjob_bandit24
leviathan5_cleanup  otw-tmp-dir

bandit21@bandit:/etc/cron.d$ cat cronjob_bandit23
@reboot bandit23 /usr/bin/cronjob_bandit23.sh  &> /dev/null
* * * * * bandit23 /usr/bin/cronjob_bandit23.sh  &> /dev/null
```
